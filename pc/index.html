NorMacroPad for Windows

The free program that presses the keys you tap in the NorMacroPad phone app. Windows 10 and 11.

Get it from Microsoft Store Download for Windows

If Windows says it “protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Then

  1. Open NorMacroPad. If Windows asks, allow it on private networks so your phone can reach it.
  2. In the phone app, tap the connection bar, then Scan the QR code shown on the PC.

Phone and PC must be on the same WiFi. Guest networks often block this; a phone hotspot works too.

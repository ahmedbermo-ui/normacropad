NorMacroPad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Applies to the NorMacroPad apps for Android and Windows.

The short version

NorMacroPad has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your pages, keys and settings stay on your phone and your own PC. We, the developer, never receive them.

What stays on your devices

  • Your pads (pages, keys, pictures, settings) are stored on the phone and copied to the NorMacroPad program on your PC, so either can restore the other.
  • The connection between phone and PC goes directly over your local network (WiFi, hotspot or USB tethering). It is not sent through the internet or through our servers; we have none. It is protected by the PIN shown on your PC.
  • What the PC reports back to the phone, such as the name of the app in front (for “this app only” keys), never leaves your network.

Permissions the phone app uses

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code on your PC. Images are processed on the phone and not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos: only when you choose a picture for a key or your logo.
  • Network: only to talk to your own PC on your local network.
  • Vibration: for the optional tap feedback.

Google services

The QR scanner uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Google may collect limited, anonymous diagnostic information about how ML Kit performs, as described in Google's ML Kit terms. Google Play and the Microsoft Store handle downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies.

Files you share

If you export a backup or share a page, it goes only where you send it (for example email or WhatsApp), under that service's privacy policy.

Children

NorMacroPad is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the phone app to remove everything on the phone. On the PC, uninstall NorMacroPad and delete the folder %APPDATA%\NorMacroPad.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub, or use the developer contact on the app's Google Play or Microsoft Store page.
